To proceed:

This blog shares children’s Islamic books and learning material intended for parents who want to teach their children with clarity, correctness, and restraint.

**What this blog is for** 

* Children’s books based on authentic Islamic sources
* Material that preserves meaning without entertainment-driven distortion
* Resources to help parents explain core concepts properly

**What this channel avoids** 

* Tasweer and character-based entertainment
* Watering down aqeedah or history, or Islaam, in general
* Content designed primarily to amuse rather than teach

**What to expect**

* Infrequent posts
* Short excerpts or announcements
* No discussion threads, no noise

This channel is intended for parents and teachers. Not for children to browse on their own.
