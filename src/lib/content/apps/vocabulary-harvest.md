---
title: "Vocabulary Harvest"
---

I built Vocabulary Harvest because I needed it myself. I found that a simple, focused vocabulary tool was what I needed, away from the bloated apps. So I recreated that idea for Arabic — and now I'm sharing it with anyone who wants to learn the language one word at a time.

This isn't another bloated app with endless subscriptions. It's a clean, offline-first tool designed to help you actually remember words and use them.

- 🌱 Learn over 800 Arabic words across 52 carefully structured lessons
- 🔤 Every word comes with full tashkeel — so you learn correct pronunciation from the start
- ⌛️ Built-in spaced repetition — the app remembers what you struggle with and brings it back at the right time
- ✦ Three practice modes — Recall, Assemble, and Listen
- 💾 Export and import your progress — your learning stays yours
