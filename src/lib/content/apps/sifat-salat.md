title: "Ṣifat Ṣalāt"
---

Ṣalāh is the pillar of the religion. But many English-speaking Muslims never learned exactly how to pray — with accurate knowledge, word by word.

This app walks you through the entire prayer — from the opening takbīr to salām — based on _Talkhīṣ Ṣifat Ṣalāt an-Nabī_ ﷺ by al-Albānī.

**What you get:**

- 📖 Full prayer with Arabic text, transliteration, and English meaning
- 🎯 4 practice activities: Meaning, Recall, Structure, Spelling
- 🔊 Custom audio recordings for every phrase
- 🌙 Light & dark themes, customizable fonts
- 📱 Works offline — install on your phone, tablet, or desktop
- ✨ No account, no subscription.

**Who it's for:** Converts, beginners, parents teaching children — anyone who wants to pray with confidence.

An initial version. More features planned, if Allāh wills.
