Treat yourself just like a child who needs to eat his veggies.

"No moooooom…broccoli doesn't taste good! I hate broccoli!!! I want donuts dipped in mountains of choco…!"

"If you eat dem steamed veggies you'll be healthy and won't have to have medicine and syringes".

"Mommy, Mustapha eats all the donuts he wants and he's stronger than me!"

"Not for long if he keeps it that way…but fine, if you eat dem steamed veggies I'll get you half a donut dipped in choco then".

"No, a full donut!"

"Final offer! Take it or leave it".

"Ok…"

So, being a Muslim, you're that child, who finds it hard and boring to practice Islam.

Read the Quran?
Learn how to pray?
Learn purification?
Don't do that haram…even if some say it is not and do it themselves? Remember how Mustapha eats junk food and he's toooootally fine!

Now, please don't think, "aha! So a little haram to help myself worship Allah! Wow, Islam is so easy just like they said!"

Nah man…sure the example could be better (even if analogies are not expected to be an exact match), but the donut is what you enjoy in life from that which Allah made halal.

If you keep doing pure acts of worship, you will get bored and tired, that's totally normal, and we all have that, which is why Allah gave us abundant halal ways we use to freshen up and have some fun and leisure time.

You might like to cook, hike, run, swim, read a book, stare at plants (yeah why not, just don't look weird while at it).

So an hour here, an hour there, and look at that! You had a good day!

You might also be thinking, "why is she feeding her child boring steamed broccoli?! She could make it delicious and fun and it'd still be healthy!"

Well, look at that! Aren't you creative!

So, let's use our creativity to help ourselves be better Muslims, that's your capital, your main win, why not put your premium effort there?!

Or is Mustapha still healthy when he eats all the donuts he wants…?

\*innocent wide grin\*

— Al-Qaddari, an ex-child who surprised adults by eating veggies and asking for them as a toddler.
